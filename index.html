Who We Are

The Studio

Nexus Gaming Studios is an independent Roblox development studio building structured, immersive roleplay experiences driven by teamwork, community engagement, and a commitment to quality.

Our main server, Project: Westmont, is set in the fictional State of Westmont — a custom state, county and township created from scratch, not modeled on any real city.

We operate like a professional studio. Every department, staff member and decision puts our community and players first.

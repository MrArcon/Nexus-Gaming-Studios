Who We Are

The Studio

Nexus University Gaming Studios is an independent Roblox development studio dedicated to crafting structured, immersive roleplay experiences driven by teamwork and community engagement.

We believe great roleplay comes from great infrastructure — clear departments, active moderation, and a player base that takes the world seriously. That's what we're building.

Our flagship project, Project: Los Angeles, is an open-world, Los Angeles–inspired environment pushing the limits of what Roblox roleplay can be.

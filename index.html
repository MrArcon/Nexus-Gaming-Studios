Who We Are

The Studio

Nexus Gaming Studios is an independent Roblox development studio dedicated to crafting structured, immersive roleplay experiences driven by teamwork, community engagement, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

Our flagship title, Project: Los Angeles, is a realistic open-world roleplay experience set in a Los Angeles-inspired environment — one of the most ambitious RP projects on the platform.

We operate as a professional studio. Every department, every staff member, and every decision is made with our community and players in mind first.
